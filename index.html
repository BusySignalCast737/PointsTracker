Offline & privacy

This single-file version stores data locally in your browser on this device and does not send it to a server. Open it once while online or save it to Files and open it in a browser, then test it in Airplane Mode. Browser file-opening behavior varies; for dependable offline use, the next step is hosting it as an installable web app (PWA) and adding it to your Home Screen.

Keep regular backups. Clearing browser data, deleting the saved file, or changing browsers can make locally stored data unavailable.
